## Bug

Customer one-edit typos in known removal/transport terms could be understood by the LLM but rejected by deterministic evidence grounding, causing the same qualification question to repeat. Reproduced with `iano` and `pianow` while answering the heavy/awkward item question.

## Fix

Added bounded one-edit typo normalisation in the release reducer. The raw customer message remains unchanged in persisted audit data; only evidence supplied to the reducer is normalised. Geography is deliberately excluded: towns, addresses and postcodes are never fuzzy-corrected.

Collision guards prevent common real words such as `left`, `chain`, `cable`, `soft`, `boxer`, `able`, `stable`, and `tablet` from being silently converted to removal terms.
